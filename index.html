How these fit together

Pick one, combine a few, or keep what you have.

Alongside your current site

Ideas 1 and 3 can sit next to your WordPress site and Punchpass as extra pages. Nothing you use today has to change.

As a replacement

Idea 2 is a full new website. Ideas 1 and 3 slot into it as its “New here” and “Events” pages.

Punchpass stays

Every “Book” and “Buy” button in these mockups opens your real Punchpass pages, so schedules, passes and payments stay where you manage them now.

These are ideas to react to, not a finished product. If any of them resonate, tell Dylan which parts you like and what you’d change. Adjustments are easy, and scope and price are always agreed with you before anything goes live.
