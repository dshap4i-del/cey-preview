Before your first class

  • Book ahead, at least 2 hours before a studio class. You can cancel up to 2 hours before.
  • Sign your new student waiver before you come.
  • When you arrive, sign in, silence your phone and leave it with your things in the locked hallway.
  • Please skip perfume and fragranced lotion. Essential oils are sometimes offered and always optional.
  • Step over or around other people’s mats.

Practicing from home

  • Book at least 1 hour before an online class.
  • Dim the lights, close the door, light a candle, turn on ambient music.
  • Gather props: blankets, pillows, towels, a strap (a belt or scarf works), blocks (large books) and an eye pillow (a scarf or hand towel).
  • The studio camera is placed so in-studio students aren’t seen online.

Bringing teens?

Ages 12–15 are welcome in all classes with an adult. Teens 16 and older can come on their own once a parent or guardian has signed a waiver.
