What this is

A full replacement for coconutelephantyoga.com, with your schedule, classes, teachers, services, passes, events and directions on one calm site.

How it would work day to day

  • You keep adding classes, events and passes in Punchpass, exactly as now.
  • Visitors see this week’s classes on the site; every Book or Buy button opens your Punchpass page to register and pay.
  • Text, photos and teachers come from you; how updates are made would be agreed with you.
  • Ideas 1 and 3 can become its “New here” and “Events” pages.

Why it’s designed this way

Your current site sends people away to Punchpass to see the schedule, still lists two past events, has a NEW STUDENT button that opens an error page, and has an unlinked About page still showing the template’s sample text.
