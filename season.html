What this is

A seasonal page for workshops and circles, with a calendar, full event details, one-tap calendar adds and a gift card idea.

How it would work day to day

  • You create each event in Punchpass as you do now.
  • It appears here with its photo, date, teacher, price and your description.
  • People tap “Get a ticket”, which opens that event’s Punchpass page to pay.
  • Past events drop off on their own, so the page stays current.

Why it’s designed this way

Today your events are spread across the home page, the Events and Training page and Punchpass, and two that already happened (Nov 6 and Nov 14) were still listed on Nov 15.

?How “Add to calendar” worksUnder every event

What this is

Two buttons on each event that put it in the visitor’s own calendar.

How it would work day to day

  • “Add to calendar” saves a calendar file that opens in most phone and computer calendars.
  • “Google Calendar” opens Google with the event filled in.
  • Each entry has the date, time, studio address and the ticket link. Nothing comes to you; it all happens on their phone.

Why it’s designed this way

Events are a few weeks out, so having the date and ticket link in their own calendar makes it easier to remember and come back to buy.

Pick a class or event, add a note, and save a card to send by text or tuck into a holiday card. In a live version the gift would be paid for the way Mary Beth chooses, for example through Punchpass.

?How “Give a class” worksA holiday idea, and what would still need deciding

What this is

A way for someone to give a class, a pass or an event ticket, with a card they can text or print.

How it would work day to day

  • The giver picks a gift, types names and a note, and saves the card image.
  • They send it by text or tuck it into a holiday card.
  • In a live version, how the gift is paid for and redeemed would be set up with you, for example as a Punchpass pass bought for the recipient.

Why it’s designed this way

Your Holiday Serenity event invites people to “give yourself the gift of a little peace,” and we didn’t see a gift option on your Punchpass passes page.
