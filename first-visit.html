Coming to the studio

  • Where: 825 Grove Road, Suite 12, Midlothian, in the Grove Road Office Complex near Midlothian Turnpike and Courthouse Rd.
  • Register ahead: at least two hours before an in-studio class. You can cancel up to two hours before.
  • When you arrive: sign in. Put your phone on silent and leave it in the locked hallway with your things.
  • Skip the scent: no perfume or fragranced lotion. Essential oils are sometimes offered and always optional.
  • Mat manners: step over or around other people’s mats.

Practicing from home

  • Register ahead: at least one hour before an online class. You can cancel up to two hours before.
  • Set the room: dim the lights, close the door, light a candle, turn on ambient music.
  • Props from home: blankets, pillows and towels. A belt, tie, scarf or robe strap works as a strap, large books or canned goods as blocks, and a scarf, sock or hand towel as an eye pillow.
  • Zoom acting up? Log out and back in, close other tabs, restart and plug in your device, and turn your device’s sound down if audio is off.
  • Privacy: the studio camera is placed so in-studio students aren’t seen online.
